How we work this out

We assume that 60% of your electricity goes to heating, that the pump takes over 60% of that heating, and that it gives 3 kWh of heat for each kWh it uses over a year. The home visit gives a figure for your house.

From the first visit to a warm living room in about a week

Most of that week is waiting for a free day in the calendar. The work itself takes an afternoon.

  1. The visit

    45 minutes

    An installer comes to your home, measures the rooms and finds the best wall for the indoor and outdoor units. The visit is free, and you are not obliged to buy.

  2. The price

    Within two working days

    You get one written price. It covers the pump, the fitting, pipes, brackets and VAT, and it does not change on the day.

  3. The fitting

    Four to six hours

    Two installers put up both units, drill one hole through the outer wall and seal it. Before they leave, they show you the remote and the app.

Questions we often get before a visit

If yours is missing, write it in the form below and the installer will answer it when we call.

How loud is it?

The indoor unit runs at 19 dB on its lowest setting, which is quieter than a whisper. The outdoor unit sounds about like a fridge when you stand beside it. We place it away from bedroom windows, both yours and your neighbour's.

Does it work when it is really cold?

Yes. All three models are built for Nordic winters and give heat down to −25 °C. They give less heat per kWh on the coldest days, so keep your panel heaters as a back-up.

Can I get support from Enova?

No. Enova gives no support for air-to-air heat pumps. Some other heating systems do qualify, and Enova's website lists them.

How long does a heat pump last?

Twelve to fifteen years with a service every second year. A service takes an hour and costs 1,490 kr.

Can it cool the house in summer?

Yes. The same pump cools in summer, and it dries the air on damp days.

Do I need to be home during the fitting?

Only at the start and towards the end. We agree on where the units go when we arrive, and we show you how everything works before we leave.
